Paid Marketing · Research

What people search for, and who to aim at, that the account is not covering yet.

Give it a client: what it sells, a website, and if there is one, the ad account. On Google it asks Google Keyword Planner what people search for around the client's services, its site and its competitors' sites, then checks every idea against the keywords and negative keywords the account already has, so nothing is suggested twice. On Meta, where ads are not bought on keywords, it looks the business up in Meta's own list of interests, reads which interests the ad sets already aim at and how each one is doing, and plans the audiences to test. Claude judges what fits; every number is Google's or Meta's. Download it as Excel, CSV or PDF, or as a file for Google Ads Editor.
