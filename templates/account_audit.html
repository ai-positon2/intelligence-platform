Is the account set up to win? Find out before you build on it.

Pick a Google Ads account, a Meta ad account or one of each. The agent reads it live and holds it against a written checklist: the settings, the conversion tracking, the links, the structure and bidding, the keywords and search terms, the ads and the landing pages for Google, and the ad account, the pixel, the campaigns and the ads for Meta. Every check has a severity and a result, and anything it could not read says so instead of passing or failing. You get a score, the checks to fix first, and the full list to download.

Read only: nothing is changed in either platform. One account at a time (an MCC is not audited). The read is saved for an hour, so the same audit opens at once, and Refresh asks the platform again.
