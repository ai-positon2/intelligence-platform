Paid Marketing · Research

A clear picture of the business, before any audit or strategy.

Give it a client: a website, a brief, and if there is one, the Google Ads account. It reads the site and what the site is set up to measure, what customers and the press say, what the brand posts on social media, what the account has already spent and a few competitors, then writes up the business: what it sells, what sets it apart and what proves it, who buys and why, what it means for paid media, and the questions to put to the client. Download it as Excel, CSV or PDF.
