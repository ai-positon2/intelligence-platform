Paid Marketing · Build and launch

Ad copy that fits the limits, and only says what the client can stand behind.

Say what the ads are for and pick the formats: Google Search, Performance Max, Demand Gen, Responsive Display, Meta feed, Meta Stories and Reels. It reads the client's website and, if you give one, the Google Ads account, then Claude writes each format from only that and the facts you confirmed. Every line is then checked by rules, not by the AI: the character limit, Google's and Meta's ad rules, repeats, competitor names, and any claim (a price, "free", years in business, a rating, "best") that the evidence does not hold is marked Confirm instead of being let through. Formats with an image or a video come with a creative brief. Download Excel, CSV or PDF, or the search ad as a file for Google Ads Editor.
