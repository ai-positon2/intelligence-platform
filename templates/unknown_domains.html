Paid Marketing · Account safety

An ad that points somewhere it should not.

Every 4 hours the agent reads every enabled ad in every Google Ads and Meta Ads account, checks that its link works and does not redirect, and checks where it sends people against the domain database below. An address nobody approved is either an ad launched under the wrong account or the first sign of an account that has been taken over, and either way the team hears about it by email and Slack. It only reads. It never pauses or changes an ad.

The domain database starts empty, so the first scan finds every address in every account. Look through what each account points at today and approve the ones that are right: Trust what the accounts use today adds every domain below to the database, for the account that uses it, and from then on anything new alerts. Trusting everything also trusts an account that has already been taken over, so look through the list first.

Findings
URL Checker

Every scan also opens the final URL of every enabled ad and lists the ones that are broken (page not found, a server error, a domain that does not resolve, a bad certificate, no answer) or that redirect somewhere else. New ones are emailed as a sheet so the affected campaigns can be paused and fixed. A change that only moves a page from http to https, adds or drops www or a trailing slash is not a redirect. A site that refuses automated checks is counted as not verified, never as broken.

Domain database

A domain, and who may send people to it: one account, a client (every account under its manager account) or everyone. An entry for example.com covers www.example.com and every other subdomain.

Import a list, or export this one

Columns: domain, then account or client (a name or an id; blank means everyone), then note. Upload a CSV, paste it, or give a Google Sheet link shared as anyone with the link can view.

Where alerts go

Each finding is alerted once, as one email and one Slack message per scan. The Slack bot has to be a member of the channel.

Recent scansthe last 10

Read only: the agent never pauses an ad or changes an account, so a suspected takeover is for a person to act on. It reads the final URLs of enabled ads (and the sitelinks, Performance Max asset groups and tracking templates around them) on Google Ads and the website of every active Meta ad. Paused ads, who changed what, new users and billing changes are not read. Addresses on Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and the app stores are never findings.
