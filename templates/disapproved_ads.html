Ads that are not running.

Once a day the agent reads every active ad account for ads Google or Meta has disapproved and tells the team by email and Slack, with the policy behind each one and a sheet to work from. It looks only at what is live: the campaign, the ad group and the ad must all be enabled, so paused campaigns and ad groups never add noise. It only reads. It never pauses, edits or appeals an ad.
