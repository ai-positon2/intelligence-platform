Where they lead

The honest part: where the incumbents win.

AI answer visibility is a real, crowded category

Dedicated monitors like Profound and AthenaHQ, plus modules inside Ahrefs and Semrush, track AI answer visibility well. We are an early mover here, not the only one. Our edge is connecting it to signals and execution, not the tracking alone.

The deepest SEO datasets are theirs

Ahrefs and Semrush have spent over a decade building backlink and keyword indexes larger than anything we run. If your single need is raw index depth, that is genuinely their strength, and we will say so.

Some go broader than you would expect

Semrush ships a visitor-ID pixel and full ad research. Profound and AthenaHQ can draft content. They are not one-trick tools. What none of them do is tie signals, visitors and AI visibility into one scored worklist that a team then acts on.

We would rather tell you this up front than have you find it later. The case for Intelligence is not that the others are bad. It is that none of them connect the finding, the deciding and the doing in one place, on your own accounts.
