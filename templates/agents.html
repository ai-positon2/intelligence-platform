Privacy & data

Privacy at Intelligence.

A plain-language summary of how we handle intent data, consent and your information. For our full policy or a Data Processing Agreement, just ask.

Our approach

Intelligence by Position2 helps revenue teams spot buying intent from publicly available and first-party signals. We collect only what’s needed to surface and explain those signals, and we aim to handle every data point lawfully, fairly and transparently.

What we process

Depending on your configuration, this can include business firmographic data, publicly available company news and activity, signals from your own website and CRM, and engagement data you are entitled to use. We do not seek out sensitive personal categories.

Website de-anonymization & consent

Where visitor identification is enabled, it is intended to be used on traffic you are permitted to process, with appropriate notice and consent in place on your properties. You are responsible for your site’s consent banner and privacy notice; we provide controls and guidance to help you stay compliant with GDPR, CCPA and similar regimes.

Your choices

You can request access, correction, export or deletion of data we process on your behalf, and you can disable specific signal sources at any time. We honour valid data-subject and opt-out requests.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is restricted on a least-privilege basis, and handling is logged and reviewed. We work with vetted sub-processors under appropriate agreements.

Contact

Questions, exports or deletion requests: privacy@position2.com. This summary is provided for transparency and is not a substitute for our full, contractually binding policy.
