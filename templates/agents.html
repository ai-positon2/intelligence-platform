{% elif page == 'signals' %}
What we listen for

Buying signals,
ranked by what matters.

We track distinct signal types across three priority tiers. High-tier signals come from curated, authenticated sources; medium and low tiers are mined continuously from the open web and news.

High priority
Funding Round

New capital raised — fresh budget and a mandate to grow.

C-Suite Join

A new executive arrives with a fresh agenda and new vendors.

C-Suite Exit

Leadership departs — strategy and priorities are in flux.

Acquisition / M&A

A deal reshapes the org chart, stack and spending.

IPO Signal

Going public — scrutiny, scale and new investment.

Subsidiary Change

A new entity or restructure opens fresh territory.

Medium priority
Product Launch

A new product means new go-to-market motion to support.

Partnership

A new alliance signals expansion and shifting needs.

Creative Hiring

Hiring marketers and creatives — investment in growth.

Low priority
News Mention

General coverage that keeps an account warm and in view.

Every signal is scored as type_weight × severity × recency, with a bonus when several signals stack on one account — so the most urgent opportunities rise to the top automatically.
