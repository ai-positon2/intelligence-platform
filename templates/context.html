{% elif chapter.slug == 'why-we-built-it' %}
Before

Great agents, no front door

Arena is packed with powerful AI agents, but it's an internal system that only Position² employees can reach. There was no friendly, interactive front-end for it, so the people who needed these agents most still couldn't actually use them.

→
After

A home our team can use

So we built Intelligence: a beautiful, interactive homepage for the whole fleet. Most agents are still powered by Arena under the hood; this platform is the front-end that lets our team pick one and put it to work.

Arena is the engine. Intelligence is the cockpit, where our team can actually fly it.
