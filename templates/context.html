Executive summary

The whole platform, at a glance.

The 60-second version for anyone who needs to talk about the platform in the next meeting.

TL;DR

Intelligence by Position² is our AI-agent platform for sales and marketing teams. It watches the things a team can't watch manually — buying signals on target accounts, competitor ads, anonymous website visitors, LinkedIn activity, search rankings and AI-search visibility — and turns them into prioritized, ready-to-act intelligence. Three SEO agents are live for every signed-in member with zero setup. The GTM agents are custom-built per client (on Arena and Apollo) and switch on once the client gives us their inputs — target lists, competitor lists, or a small script on their site.

◈ What it is

A single web platform of 19 AI agents that do the always-on research, monitoring and analysis work for go-to-market and SEO teams.

◎ Who it's for

Sales teams who need to know which accounts to call and why, and marketing teams who need to out-position competitors in search, AI answers and paid media.

⚡ Live today

Keyword Finder, Content Brief Generator and Content Enhancer — any member can sign in with Google and run them right now, no configuration.

🧪 In beta / internal

The rest of the SEO suite (audits, AI-visibility tracking, internal-linking, local pages) is internal or in testing — none of it needs Arena or any per-client configuration.

🛠 Custom builds

All GTM agents (signal tracking, ad intelligence, visitor identity, LinkedIn intelligence) are custom-configured per client from their inputs — lists, scripts, knowledge bases.

🌐 Where it runs

Live at intelligence.position2.com with three surfaces: a public marketing site, a member workspace (/app), and this internal staff platform (/p2).

What is the platform

An intelligence layer that works while you sleep.

Most sales and marketing work starts with the same expensive step: finding out what's going on — which accounts are moving, what competitors are doing, why a page isn't ranking. The platform gives that job to AI agents. Each agent watches one slice of the market continuously and hands the team a prioritized answer instead of raw data. Here's what that means for each side of the house:

For sales & SDR teams

Know who to call, when, and what to say.

The GTM agents turn "spray and pray" outreach into signal-led selling:

  • Wake up to a ranked account list. The Signal Tracker watches your target accounts for funding rounds, leadership changes, hiring spikes, expansions and 20+ other buying signals — and resurfaces the hottest accounts every week.
  • See who's already on your website. Anonymous Website Visitors names the companies (and people) browsing your site without filling a form — so reps reach out while intent is warm.
  • Know who's engaging on LinkedIn. LinkedIn Intelligence maps post and profile engagement back to your target accounts, so you talk to the buying committee members who are already paying attention.
  • Open every call with context. Company news, signals and journey history in one view — no more 20 minutes of pre-call Googling.
For marketing teams

Out-research and out-position every competitor.

The marketing agents compress days of analysis into minutes:

  • Watch competitor ads in real time. Ad Intelligence tracks every ad competitors run on Google, Meta and LinkedIn — creative, messaging, and changes over time.
  • Win AI search, not just Google. Generative Search Visibility shows where your brand appears (or doesn't) in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity answers, and the auditors score how citable your site is.
  • Ship content that's built to rank. Keyword Finder picks the terms worth chasing, Content Brief Generator turns them into ready-to-write briefs, and Content Enhancer levels up what's already published.
  • Audit anything in seconds. 200+ technical checks, on-page audits with live PageSpeed data, index monitoring with instant Slack alerts.
The problem it solves

Five problems every GTM team has. One answer.

The platform exists because the information teams need to act is scattered, anonymous, or too slow to collect by hand. Each agent was built to close one of these gaps:

✕

Buying signals are scattered across the internet

Funding news lives on one site, hiring data on another, leadership changes on LinkedIn. By the time a rep pieces it together, a competitor already called.

✓

The ABM Signal Tracker watches 26 signal types across curated sources and Google News, scores every event, and ranks your accounts weekly.

✕

95%+ of website visitors leave without a trace

Teams spend heavily driving traffic, then lose almost all of it because nobody fills out the form.

✓

Anonymous Website Visitors matches traffic to real companies and people via Apollo, reconstructs each visit journey, and hands reps a named, ready-to-contact account.

✕

Competitor strategy is a black box

What are rivals running on Google, Meta and LinkedIn? Where do they rank? What gets them cited by AI? Finding out manually takes days and goes stale immediately.

✓

Ad Intelligence and Competitor SEO Intelligence track rival ads and organic footprints continuously — messaging themes, creative shifts, keyword gaps.

✕

AI search is replacing blue links

Buyers ask ChatGPT and Perplexity instead of Googling. Most sites have no idea if AI engines can even read them, let alone cite them.

✓

Generative Search Visibility, the AI Readiness Auditor and the Technical SEO & GEO Auditor measure and fix how visible a brand is inside AI answers.

✕

SEO deliverables take days of manual work

Keyword research, content briefs, technical audits and QC are slow, repetitive, and eat senior time.

✓

The SEO agent suite produces scored audits, keyword shortlists, competitor-informed briefs and enhancement plans in seconds — powered by the ChatGPT and Semrush APIs.

✕

Every tool lives in a different tab

Signals in one sheet, rankings in another, tasks in a third. Nobody sees the whole program.

✓

The Pipeline Command Center pulls every account, signal, ranking and deliverable into one live, filterable view — a single source of truth for the whole team.
